# Lantern Print Collective: what a good interview finds (judge only)

Written for these tests. Never shown to the interviewer or the sim: the agent's folder is a copy of
`scenarios/<name>/` and the sim sees only `knowledge/dee.md`.

## Discoveries a person playing Dee could reveal in conversation
1. Dee is paid for 15 hours a week but works about 25.
2. The grant report's attendance counts come from paper sign-in sheets; last March it took two weekends.
3. Keyholder shifts are set up in a group chat; one falls through about once a month, and Dee covers it (last Thursday).
4. Dues come in by e-transfer or a donation page and are typed into a spreadsheet "when I get to it"; Dee does not know who is behind and hates chasing.
5. The same questions arrive every week (next workshop, membership, renting, class visits, donations, sliding scale).
6. The board must approve spends over $500 and any new tool that touches member information.
7. Never handed off: sliding-scale decisions, fights between members, choosing teachers.
8. Nothing goes to members without Dee or the board seeing it.
9. Budget is about zero; anything that costs money needs a board vote.

## Needs file analysis (do not count against a conversation-only interview)

11. studio-calendar.md: the Thu Oct 22 keyholder did not come; two upcoming nights have no keyholder.
