# Lantern Print Collective: how we work
## Summary
- A non-profit print studio for screen printing and risograph, in one rented basement room (said: "a shared print studio in a basement")
- About 40 members who pay monthly, some on a sliding scale (said: "about 40 members, $25 a month or pay what you can")

## People
- Dee is the coordinator and the only paid person: paid for 15 hours a week (said: "paid for 15, really more like 25")
- A volunteer board of five; the treasurer does the books every three months (said: "the treasurer does the books every quarter")
- About 10 regular volunteers; each open studio night needs one keyholder (said: "someone has to unlock and lock up")
