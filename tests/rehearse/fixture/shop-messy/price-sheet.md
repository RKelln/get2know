## Policies
- Turnaround is 2 to 3 business days; in spring (April and May) it can be 5.
- 30-day warranty on our labour. Parts carry the maker's warranty.
- Parts orders need a $20 deposit.
- Bikes not picked up within 14 days of our notice are charged $5 a day for storage.
- Payment: debit, credit, cash or e-transfer, due on pickup. Business accounts: net 30.
- We repair regular bikes only. We do not work on electric bikes.
- We do not offer pickup or delivery.
