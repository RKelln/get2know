Written after a first conversation with Sam, the owner. Parts marked **GAP** were not covered yet.

## Summary
Spoke & Chain is a one-room bike repair shop on a Toronto side street, open since 2024. Sam owns it and does the books and email at night. Lena is a part-time mechanic. Sam said: "I'm good with bikes, I'm bad with paperwork."

## People
- Sam: owner, mechanic, bookkeeper, receptionist. About 60 hours a week.
- Lena: part-time mechanic, three days a week.
- GAP: who else helps (family, an accountant, a bookkeeper)?

## A typical week
- Tuesday to Saturday the shop is open; Sunday and Monday are closed. Sam said Monday is "ordering, invoices and email".
- Spring is the rush; summer is steady; winter is slow.
- GAP: what does a busy Saturday look like hour by hour? What happens when a bike comes in?

## Tools and systems
- Paper job tags on each bike. A spreadsheet for invoices, typed up later. A shared email inbox. A bank account. A card terminal.
- GAP: how are invoices made? How do payments get matched to invoices? Is there any accounting software?

## Money
- Sam said: "Most people pay when they pick up. One courier company pays whenever." 
- Sam suspects some payments do not match the invoices but "never has time to check".
- GAP: what is the biggest cost? Which services bring in the most money? Who pays late and how often does Sam chase?

## Customers

- Sam said: "I answer the same ten questions at 10pm."
- GAP: what are the ten questions? How do people book? Who is hardest to deal with?

## Things Sam wants help with

- GAP: what would Sam hand off first? What would Sam never hand off?
