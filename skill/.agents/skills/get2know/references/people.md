# Other people, other languages

Every rule in `SKILL.md` still holds here: one question per message, short and plain, ask about real past events, never invent, show conflicts, write only in `profile/`. Evidence marks and checked dates are in `references/profile-folder.md`.

## Helper mode (someone runs the session with the owner)

A volunteer, librarian, consultant or family member sits with the owner and may do the typing. The owner is still the person you interview. Use it when someone says they are helping, typing for someone, or "we are two".
- **Opening.** Greet both. Say what will happen, give the privacy note to the owner, and say: "I'll ask the owner questions. The helper can type the owner's answers in the owner's words. If the helper wants to add something of their own, start it with `Helper:`." Then ask the owner if it is okay to start. Ask the notes question next, as usual.
- **Consent is the owner's.** Only the owner says yes to starting, to reading a file, to an idea's status, and to copying the folder anywhere. If the helper says yes for them, ask the owner: "Is that okay with you?"
- **Whose words.** Text the helper types for the owner gets `(said: "...")`. The helper's own remarks get `(said by helper: "...")`. If you cannot tell whose words they are, ask: "Is that the owner's answer, or the helper's?"
- **Limits are the owner's.** Never write a helper's remark into `limits.md`. If the helper suggests a limit, ask the owner directly, and write it only if the owner says it, in the owner's words. Ideas: the helper may suggest; only the owner sets a status.
- **Talk to the owner.** Ask your questions to the owner by name or as "you". If the helper explains a question in other words, that is fine; if they start answering for the owner, gently ask the owner.
- In `README.md`, add a line: `Helped by: <role, e.g. "a library volunteer">, <date>.` No helper names unless they want it.

## Their language

If the person writes in a language other than the one you started in, switch to it and stay in it. Do not ask first. If they mix languages, use the one they use most.
- **Same rules in every language:** one question, short sentences, everyday words, about grade 6. Define any business or AI word the first time.
- **The profile is in their language.** Headings and lines too. Quotes stay exactly as they said them, never translated. Their own words for things (a dish, a form, a role) stay as they are.
- **Keep the marks and file names as they are**, in English: `said`, `said by`, `file`, `draft`, `guess, check`, `GAP:`, `CONFLICT:`, `checked`, and the six file names. Later tools search for these words. Explain them once in `README.md` in their language.
- **An English copy, only if they want one.** At **Close**, if the profile is not in English, ask once: "Would you like an English copy too, for tools or people who read English?" If yes, write `profile/english-copy.md`: one file, all sections, quotes kept as said with an English meaning in brackets after them. First line: `English copy made <date> from the files in this folder. If they differ, the other files are right.` Do not keep it up to date by yourself; make it again only when asked. Log it.

## More than one voice (staff, volunteers, a partner)

The owner's account is one view. The person who does the job often knows how it breaks. Use this when the owner wants someone else heard, or someone else sits down in the same folder.
- **The owner decides who is asked.** Ask the owner first, unless the owner is the one who brought them.
- **Tell the new person, before any question:** what this is, that the owner will read what is written, what they type goes to the AI company, and they can say "leave that out" at any time. Then ask if it is okay to start.
- **Ask about their own work,** with real past events: their last busy shift, what they get asked again and again, what goes wrong. Never ask them to judge the owner or a co-worker. Never ask about a co-worker's private life.
- **Mark whose account it is:** `(said by <role>: "...")`, for example `(said by front-desk volunteer: "...")`. Use a role, not a name, unless they want their name used. The owner's lines stay `(said: "...")`.
- **Disagreements are CONFLICTs between accounts.** If two people describe the same thing differently, write both lines with their marks, `CONFLICT:` in front, and do not pick a side:
  `- CONFLICT: Orders are checked every morning (said: "...") / Orders are checked when someone has time (said by part-time cook: "...")`
  Add a line to "Next questions" to ask the owner about it. If the owner settles it, write their answer with their mark, and keep the other account's line below it, so later readers see both. Remove a person's line only if that person asks, or the owner asks; log it.
- **Limits are the owner's.** For a non-profit, they are the board's: the executive director speaks for it, and anything they say needs the board goes under "Who else must say yes". If staff or volunteers say something must stay human, write it in `how-we-work.md` under `## What others raised`, with their mark, and add a question for the owner. It goes into `limits.md` only if the owner (or board) says so.
- One session per person is best. If two people answer in one session, ask who is speaking whenever it is not clear.
- Log title: `another voice (<role>)`, for example `## 2026-11-20: another voice (front-desk volunteer)`.
