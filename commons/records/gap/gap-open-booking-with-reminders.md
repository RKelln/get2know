## The need

A small service business wants customers to book a time online and get a text reminder, on a cheap host, with fully open software.

## Why it is a gap

Cal.diy, the open edition of Cal.com, says it is for personal, non-production use. Easy!Appointments is open and maintained but, as far as checked, has no built-in text reminders (none found in its README or code). Text messages need a paid sending service either way (an openness-tier-2 connector).

## Who could take it

A hackathon or workshop cohort: add text reminders to an existing open booking tool, upstream.
